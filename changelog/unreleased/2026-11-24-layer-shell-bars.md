- Wayland overlays can be desktop panels. `OverlayOptions::wayland_layer` picks the layer-shell layer (`WaylandLayer::Background`, `Bottom`, `Top` or `Overlay`, the default and what every overlay used before); `OverlayOptions::exclusive_zone` reserves space along an edge so the compositor keeps windows out of it (`ExclusiveZone::Reserve(points)`), or extends under space others reserve (`IgnoreOthers`); and `OverlayPlacement::Bar { edge, thickness, margin }` docks an overlay along a whole edge (`BarEdge::Top`, `Bottom`, `Left` or `Right`), stretched to the display's length by the compositor. Other backends ignore the layer and the zone and place a bar as a window of the display's full length. `OverlayOptions` gains two fields and `OverlayPlacement` a variant, so a literal without `..Default::default()` and an exhaustive `match` need updating. `examples/layer-bar` is a top bar that reserves its height and toggles a popup.
