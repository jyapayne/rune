- **`rune::session_lock`: a lock screen on Wayland's `ext-session-lock-v1`.** `AppHandle::lock_session(on_change)` asks the compositor to lock the session and returns a `SessionLock` (`state`, `unlock`); `ConfiguredOverlay::session_lock(&lock)` makes an overlay on a display of its own that lock's surface there: the compositor sizes it to the display, gives it the keyboard (`KeyPolicy::Exclusive`), and it is never unmapped (a hide destroys it) or prewarmed. `on_change` hears `LockState::Locked`, then `Unlocked` after `unlock` or `Finished` when the compositor refuses or ends the lock (`destroy` before `locked`, `unlock_and_destroy` after, as the protocol asks), or `Unsupported`; the lock's surfaces close as it ends. An unlock asked for before `locked` waits for it. Only `unlock` unlocks: dropping every handle, a panic or the process ending sends nothing, and the session stays locked. Lock surfaces live on their overlays' own queues (`get_lock_surface` is sent on the overlay's connection), and nothing is committed before the first `configure` is acked. `ConfiguredOverlay::on_keyboard_state` hears Caps Lock, Num Lock and the layout name (`KeyboardState`) on Wayland layer and lock surfaces. Tests run against a fake compositor that enforces the protocol's errors; `examples/session-lock` locks with a passphrase and a surface per display, including one plugged in while locked.
