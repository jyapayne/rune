- Linux/Wayland: `rune::set_parent_window(identifier, modal)` makes an app's windows children of another app's window, named as xdg-desktop-portal names it (`wayland:<xdg-foreign handle>`), through `zxdg_importer_v2`, and modal to it through `xdg_wm_dialog_v1` where the compositor has it; how a portal backend's dialog stays with the app that asked. Other identifiers and platforms leave windows as they are.
