- **`rune::capture`: screen and window capture on Wayland, for previews and a frozen screen.** A `Capturer` keeps its own Wayland connection on a thread of its own (no window needed, nothing blocks the UI thread) and captures an output by connector name or a window by its Hyprland address (`hyprctl clients -j`'s `address`, matched through `ext-foreign-toplevel-list-v1` and `hyprland-toplevel-mapping-v1`), with `ext-image-copy-capture-v1` where the compositor offers it and `wlr-screencopy-unstable-v1` (outputs) or `hyprland-toplevel-export-v1` (windows) otherwise. `Capturer::shot` returns one `Still` (packed B, G, R, A, straight alpha, upright whatever the output's transform or the buffer's row order; `crop`, `to_rgba`, `into_byte_frame`); `Capturer::stream` keeps a `NativeSurface` showing the source, asking for a frame only after the content changes, never faster than its frame-rate cap, and nothing at all while `Stream::set_shown(false)`; a resized source gets a new buffer, and a closed window ends its stream (`StreamStats::ended`). Frames travel in `wl_shm` buffers. `examples/screen-capture` saves shots, benchmarks streams and shows live previews; its README has what Hyprland 0.57.2 offers and what capture costs there.
