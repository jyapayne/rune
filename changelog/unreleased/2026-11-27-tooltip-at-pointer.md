- **Tooltips can hang under the pointer, set their first row as a title, and wrap long words.** `HelpLook::at_pointer(true)` shows a tooltip the pointer opened under where the pointer rested (16 pt below its hot spot, kept inside the window), as Qt's and Dolphin's do; one the keyboard opened, one with a `help_side` and a link's still hang from their element. `HelpLook::title(font)` sets the first of several `help_row`s in its own font. A word wider than the tooltip (a long file name or path) now breaks inside instead of running past its edge, which can change a tooltip's size, and files dragged over the window from another app start no tooltip and hide the one showing.
