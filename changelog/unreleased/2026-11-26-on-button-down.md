- **`El::on_button_down`** hears chosen mouse buttons besides the primary and secondary (the back and forward buttons, say) as they go down over the element or a descendant that doesn't take them, with `Tx::button` saying which. Unlike `pointer_buttons` with `on_press`, the element claims no other button and doesn't become a press target, so a window's root can carry the mouse's back and forward buttons without its empty space taking clicks or focus. The innermost element taking the button gets it, whichever way it takes it.
