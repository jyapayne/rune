- **`KeyPolicy::Exclusive`: an overlay that takes every key while shown, with no click.** For a launcher or a prompt. On Wayland its layer surface asks for exclusive keyboard interactivity as it shows and drops it as it hides (any layer-shell version; `OnDemand` still needs v4), so typing reaches it from its first frame. An exclusive surface hears no press outside itself, so where the compositor has `hyprland-focus-grab-v1` (Hyprland) the overlay holds a focus grab on its own surface while mapped: a press or touch anywhere else ends the grab and reaches the overlay as `ConfiguredOverlay::on_focus(false)`, once, to dismiss itself (it keeps the keys until it hides); the press the compositor sends it from outside its bounds is dropped. Without that protocol only a lost keyboard focus reports a blur. macOS, Windows and X11 treat `Exclusive` as `OnDemand`, and XWayland still can't take keys. `KeyPolicy::takes_keys()` says whether a policy takes the keyboard at all; `KeyPolicy` gains a variant, so an exhaustive `match` on it needs an arm. A layer-shell overlay's keyboard focus now follows the compositor's own enter and leave across a hide that keeps its surface. `examples/layer-bar`'s popup is such a prompt: a text field, Escape or a press outside closes it. Linux builds generate the protocol's bindings with `wayland-scanner` (already a dependency of the Wayland crates) from `crates/rune/protocols`.
